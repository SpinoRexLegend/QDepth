01 / THE QUESTION

Can reflected colour reveal the shape of the seafloor?

Traditional bathymetric surveys are accurate, but expensive and difficult to scale. This project tests whether engineered optical features—derived from blue and green bands—can estimate shallow-water depth, and whether emerging quantum models can compete with established regression techniques.

06 / READ THIS HONESTLY

A promising experiment,
not yet an operational map.

The current evidence uses one random split. Spatially adjacent observations may appear in both train and test sets, making performance optimistic.

  • Dataset provenance and depth units still need verification.
  • Spatial cross-validation is the essential next test.
  • Reported error remains roughly 5–6.5 m across a 30 m range.
